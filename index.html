About Me

From web servers to soldering irons.

Originally from Dhangadhi and currently based in Tinkune, Kathmandu, I got into tech because I liked taking things apart and figuring out how they work. Today, that translates into building complete products from scratch.

Through Nirdist Group (nirdist.com.np), I'm developing a custom multi-service platform featuring web search, authentication, real-time messaging, and WebRTC calling—built on raw JavaScript, Flutter, Cloudflare Workers, and Turso libSQL without bloated front-end frameworks.

On the hardware side, I work with ESP8266 NodeMCU boards, ESP-NOW protocols, and Raspberry Pi gateways to build off-grid sensor networks and early-warning telemetry systems.
